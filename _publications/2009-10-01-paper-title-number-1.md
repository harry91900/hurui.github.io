This paper proposes a Caputo fractional-order SEIHR model to investigate the impact of media coverage on early COVID-19 transmission dynamics in Malaysia. The model is calibrated to outbreak data from Perak, Terengganu, and Johor, and the results show that the fractional-order model outperforms the corresponding integer-order model across all error metrics.
