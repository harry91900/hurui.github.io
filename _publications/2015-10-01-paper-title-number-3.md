This paper presents a Caputo fractional-order COVID-19 model incorporating nucleic acid detection and individual protective awareness to capture memory effects and the interaction of non-pharmaceutical interventions. The study establishes the existence, non-negativity, and boundedness of solutions, derives the basic reproduction number, and analyzes the global stability of both the disease-free and endemic equilibria.

Using Malaysian COVID-19 data, the model parameters are estimated via a hybrid genetic algorithm-least squares approach. Numerical simulations show that the proposed model outperforms both integer-order and simplified fractional SEIR models in reproducing epidemic dynamics. The paper further formulates an optimal control problem and demonstrates through cost-effectiveness analysis that combined intervention strategies are more effective and economically viable than single controls.
