This paper proposes a delayed fractional-order COVID-19 SEIHRM model with media coverage to investigate epidemic dynamics in Malaysia. By incorporating both fractional-order memory effects and delay-induced behavioral response lags, the model provides a richer framework for analyzing stability, Hopf bifurcation, oscillatory behavior, and the impact of media interventions on disease transmission.
