# 📖 ABOUT ME / 关于我

I am a lecturer at Yunnan Minzu University, China. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Universiti Malaya, Malaysia, in 2026. My academic background spans mathematics, computational mathematics, and mathematical biology. My research interests focus on mathematical modelling and computational approaches for complex biological systems. Looking ahead, I aim to develop methods that integrate mathematical theory, scientific computing, and real-world data to address problems in biomedicine and public health.

我现任云南民族大学讲师，于2026年获得马来亚大学应用数学博士学位。我的学术背景涵盖数学、计算数学和数学生物学，研究兴趣主要包括复杂生物系统的数学建模与计算方法。未来，我希望进一步结合数学理论、科学计算与真实数据，探索生物医学和公共卫生领域中的相关问题。

# 🔬 CURRENT & FUTURE RESEARCH / 当前与未来研究方向

My future research will focus on developing reliable and interpretable mathematical and computational methods for biological and public-health systems. Potential directions include integrating fractional-order differential equations with physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for parameter estimation and epidemic forecasting, investigating inverse problems and parameter identifiability, and combining mechanistic models with data-driven learning. I am also interested in spline-based numerical methods and their applications to differential equations and complex dynamical systems.

未来，我将重点探索面向生物系统与公共卫生问题的可靠、可解释的数学建模与计算方法。潜在研究方向包括将分数阶微分方程与物理信息神经网络（PINNs）相结合，用于模型参数估计和传染病预测；研究反问题与参数可辨识性；以及融合机理模型与数据驱动学习方法。此外，我也希望探索样条数值方法及其在微分方程和复杂动力系统中的应用。
